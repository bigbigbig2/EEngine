vsm_util.glsl::clip_info_from_uvs active distance selector is translated to
vsm_page_table.ts. Its half extent is represented by half of the local full
clip extent. Camera/page snapping uses the local world-grid basis.
find_free_pages.glsl and allocate_pages.hlsl free/unvisited queue semantics
inform vsm_allocate_pages.ts; subgroup ballots and packed 64-bit image entries
are replaced by 64-lane prefix scans, separate ordered dispatches and unique
32-bit record writers. Admission, coarse reservation, cold ordering and
dirty-page budget policies are local, not Timberdoodle algorithms.

This notice does not claim a port of the upstream mesh shader raster pipeline.
See docs/porting/next-renderer.md for the source-to-owner map and boundaries.

Step 2 adapts gen_dirty_bit_hiz.hlsl and the directional meshlet/triangle
rejection stages in cull_and_draw_directional_pages.hlsl and
cull_and_draw_pages_shared.hlsl to shadow_tile_domain.ts and vsm_geometry.ts.
Selected dirty fine/coarse texel scales, conservative bounds, opaque non-double
backface rejection and complete-page publication are retained. Near/depth
clipping uses WebGPU hardware primitive clipping. No micro-polygon or large
triangle rejection is enabled. Native MASK sidedness is the existing EEngine
material contract, not a claim of identical upstream material behavior.
The packed selected-page pyramid, SAT page test, lossless CSR stream, source
chunk scheduling and baseline polygon clipping are local execution adaptations,
not copies of the Vulkan task/mesh framework or performance results.

src/core/raster.ts::rasterWGSL four clip distances, vertex-pulled atlas placement
